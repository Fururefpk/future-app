Trust & Safety

Verified Identities. Safer Renting.

Every landlord and tenant on our platform goes through a rigorous identity verification process to ensure a safe, trustworthy environment.

Ghana Card Verification
We verify every user’s Ghana Card against the national register before granting full access.
Biometric Face Check
Live face recognition confirms the card belongs to the person, eliminating identity fraud.
Admin Review Queue
Every verification submission is reviewed by a human admin before approval is granted.
Secure Payments
All rent transactions are recorded with idempotency keys to prevent duplicate charges.
